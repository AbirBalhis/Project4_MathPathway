Subjects Covered

Math Pathway supports students in important high school math areas through short explanations, visual learning, and cheat sheet review images.

Algebra 1

Algebra 1 introduces variables, expressions, equations, and functions. It helps students build the foundation for higher-level mathematics.

Geometry

Geometry focuses on shapes, angles, lines, area, perimeter, and reasoning with figures. It helps students understand spatial relationships and logic.

Algebra 2

Algebra 2 expands on algebra concepts by including advanced equations, functions, and problem-solving strategies used in upper-level courses.

Trigonometry

Trigonometry studies triangles, angles, sine, cosine, and tangent. It connects math to real-world measurement and applications.

Precalculus

Precalculus prepares students for advanced mathematics by reviewing functions, transformations, and analytical thinking.
